# time-lapse-artifacts

490 time-lapse recordings of one artist's traditional drawing practice across nearly two years (September 2024–August 22, 2026), with standardized acquisition since July 13, 2025. Across approximately 1.98 TB of video, the archive follows the same artist working in physical media—including pencil, ballpoint pen, fountain pen, brush, graphite, and ink—on fixed-format paper and illustration board.

## Quick Facts

- **Total videos**: 490 recordings
- **Total size**: ~1.98 TB (1,984,749,320,057 bytes)
- **Standard tier**: 362 canonical videos with complete metadata
- **Pre-Standard tier**: 128 earlier recordings with archival metadata
- **Date range**: September 2024 – August 22, 2026 (standardized since July 13, 2025)
- **Release status**: Active construction release
- **License**: CC BY-NC 4.0

## What's Included

Every published video has:
- Persistent record ID
- Content hash and byte size
- Encoded-stream metadata
- Session and provenance documentation (more complete in Standard tier)

**Note**: An earlier 2012–2016 archive of more than 8 TB exists but is not yet published in this dataset.

## What Provenance Means Here

In this dataset, **provenance** refers to the documented relationship between a physical drawing and its time-lapse record, rather than to histories of ownership or sale.
