From the artist’s account

Current working method

The pencil marks are flexible guides and are never erased. Inking proceeds left → bottom → right → top, then inward. The paper stays still while the artist moves around it.

Walls remain relatively heavier than floors. Each plane maintains an overall uniform pattern, including the line-weight taper at its bottom joins.

At the floor edge

One small action from the real-time recording.

Thicker lines reduce the negative space between them. At a continuous floor join, the artist uses this to create the appearance of shadow.

Here, short nib movements leave a broader dark patch and less negative space near the line end. The change is slight.
